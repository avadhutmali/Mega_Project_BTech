# Welcome to the IdleGrid Public Showcase Frontend!

Hello! 👋 
Your job is to build a beautiful public-facing landing page for **IdleGrid**, a mega-project that turns regular lab computers into a distributed cloud compute cluster without interrupting the students using them.

## 🎯 Your Goal
We need a visually stunning, responsive landing page that acts as the "public face" of the project. 

The main call-to-action (CTA) on this page should be a massive **"Download Setup.exe"** button. This is what students or admins will click to download our auto-installer, which configures their PC to join the grid.

## 🛠 What you need to build:
1. **Hero Section:** Catchy title (e.g., "IdleGrid: Decentralized Lab Compute"), a brief explanation, and the big `Download Setup.exe` button.
2. **How it Works Section:** 
   - Step 1: Download & Install (Requires Admin & Reboot).
   - Step 2: Auto-configures Docker & WSL in the background.
   - Step 3: Idle tracking starts — when you step away, your PC runs compute jobs!
3. **Features:** Mention things like "Zero Impact on PC Owners", "Resource Capping", and "Instant SSH Access for Jobs".
4. **Link to Dashboard:** Add a button in the nav bar that links to our internal Dashboard app (where jobs are actually submitted).

## 🎨 Design Guidelines
- Avoid basic styles! Use modern web design (glassmorphism, clean typography, dark mode, smooth micro-animations).
- You are free to use TailwindCSS, Framer Motion, or any other library you prefer. 
- Make it look premium.

Good luck!
